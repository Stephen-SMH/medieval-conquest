## Made for this game

The maps, region names, campaigns and text; the parchment, wood, stone, shield-button and plaque textures
(generated by [tools/make_theme.gd](tools/make_theme.gd)); the map and sea shaders; the banners, spear arrows,
particles and animations. Nothing is copied from *Strategy & Tactics: Medieval Wars*: its screenshots were used
only as a reference for layout and feel.
